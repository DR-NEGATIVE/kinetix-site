Kinetix Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Kinetix collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app has no internet access.

What the app stores

To work, Kinetix saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Profile: the name you enter, and optionally your age, height, body weight, training goal, experience level, training days per week and preferred weight unit.
  • Training data: your programs, custom exercises and the workouts you log (exercises, weights, reps, dates and durations).
  • Settings: for example light or dark appearance and your place in your program's rotation.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts, and the Android app does not request internet permission.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, account or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Health Connect data.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Backups, exports and imports you start

Back up now and Export to spreadsheet create a file and open Android's share menu, and only when you tap them. You decide where the file goes (for example Google Drive, email or a chat). Once you send it, the app or service you chose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Restore from backup and Import from Strong or Hevy open Android's file picker. Kinetix reads only the file you select.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Kinetix, or clearing its storage in Android's app settings, deletes everything the app stored. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Health information

Workout logs and body measurements can count as health-related information. Kinetix uses them only to show your training, progress and suggestions inside the app, on your device.

Children

Kinetix is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Kinetix is a workout tracker, not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting or sharing data, this policy and the app's Play Store data safety section will be updated before that version is released.
